Music is arguably one of mankind's greatest inventions (in my humble opinion). While I wouldn't consider myself to be a music expert by any means, I definitely enjoy various genres of music. I will be listing out the ones down below and what I like about them to the point of listing them here! Feel free to read the ones for the genres you're interested or listen to!

# Post Rock
Arguably one of my favorite music genres of all time. I love rock as a whole, and post rock just seemingly integrates rock as a genre with a self-telling narrative. The music itself is the story; not told by any lyrics, but through the collective sounds of the various instruments. They typically start slow, but they pick up the pace, eventually climbing all the way up to the climax, where you can really feel the music. It is incredibly diverse too; the amount of stories you can envision while the song plays is immense. Not to mention that since most of it is instrumental, those stories are entirely up to *you* to create. That's what I really like about post rock, and other instrumental genres. It's a common pattern you'll see, but I like post rock the most out of all of these due to its tight connection to rock as a whole.

# Indie Pop
Everyone loves indie pop; it's a popular genre for a reason. There's just something about songs that fall under this genre that invoke that sense of life. One of my biggest indie pop artists is by far Roar, known for having made *I Can't Handle Change* and *Christmas Kids*. While these songs are good, they're not my favorite Roar songs. That title goes to *The Comfort of a Laugh Track*. Not just because it sounds nice, but because of the meaning of the lyrics behind them. Unlike post rock where it is instrumental, indie pop usually consists of lyrics, and this is what helps paint the story. They're done so in a way, however, where the lyrics are vague enough to essentially wrap themselves around the story the listener creates. *The Comfort of a Laugh Track* does this especially well for me. There are many, many other indie pop songs as well that are also really good. 

# Epic Orchestral
Get used to the tonal whiplash because that is the only way to define my music taste. I've been a fan of epic orchestral ever since I was a kid; The *Battlecry* album by Two Steps From Hell (TSFH) is *still* a complete banger, even to this day for me. Epic orchestral is really good for doing what I like doing best: creating stories in my head. The blend of lyrics sung in a heavenly way along with an orchestra makes everything feel grand, like it's the culmination of a grand-scale war. It makes every-day life just feel more *alive* compared to how it usually is.
