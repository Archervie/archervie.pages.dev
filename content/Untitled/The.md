
**Waves**
After I tended to the birds I kept walking. The colors of the grass shifted through the endless gradients of possible colors; magenta grass, black grass, blue grass. I walked through it all the same until I stumbled upon the dog from before. The grass steadied under my feet, conforming itself into a deep orange. The dog meowed and it told me: "Be careful of the waves lest you be swept up in them." It then ran away, the grass' colors changing with his every step. The door then showed itself to me, the door I use for travel. I opened it, and stepped into yet another wonderful world. 

I was in a dark room, and it smelled of burned charcoal. I stepped foot deeper and the atmosphere became foggy and graphic. The air became grainy, and a still man passed me, before phasing into the wooden wall beside me. Many more came and went, phasing in and out of the room. I was curious as to what was behind that wall, the wall they went into. I walked up to the wall, hearing the faint ambience of the creaking floors. I noticed my palm had form, a rare sight, but it was distorted. I could not move my head the way I normally could; only a slight bit up, down, left and right. I couldn't even look behind me. I wore an orange shirt and I think pants, but I couldn't see. It was too dark. After the wall was another room, but the still men and women wandered about, staying still. They did not move; their arms lay still, and their legs, yet they walked. The light flicker still glowed, but I saw a bar at the bottom to tell me to grab the key. That wasn't there before. I walked around and saw a desk, with a drawer. It only took the will of my mind before the drawer opened, and inside lay a grainy key. I took it and brought it to another door, but the still men came to me and told me: "Be careful of the waves. The deeper you go, the deeper you drown." It went away back toward the other side. I then walked through the door and into. 

I stood in a sea, an orange sea, made of cheese. I feel the cheese, something I haven't before. I look at the sky and I see an array of plastics falling down onto the ground. The waves of cheese hit me, and I submerge a bit, but I go back up by my will. The plastics fall harder. The plastics come raining down. One. Two. Many. They hurt. They're small, but they hurt. Another wave sweeps me up. I begin swimming through the cheese-plastic. I swim and swim, and see an island. An island made of plastic, with a cloud of raining cheese. It has a tree, an upside down tree. Out of the cheese-plastic I see a strange creature make its way to me. They have the head of a horse, the body of a dolphin, the tail of a giraffe and the hair of a monkey. I'm going to call him Bob the Holphrafkey. He comes to me and tells me: "What you are beginning to see are the waves, the plastics, your fall. Be careful of the waves, child." Before it grew wings and flew like a bird. Guess he's a Holphrakeird now. Another huge wave of cheese-plastic hits me from the back straight into another door. 

I'm floating. A sky, I feel my weight. I use my will to keep flying. I look to my right and see I have orange wings. I see the world, the lands and sea. The trees, the grass, the small hills and the bumps. I see my body, and it's full. I see the details on my fingers, and my clothes. I fly over entire forests and reach a mountain, a nice looking mountain. I swoop down, gaze upon the city that awaits me. It's a nice looking city, with pretty lights. I walk down the mountain, and see someone, a hiker. He gasps when he sees me, but calms down after some time. I tell him: "Hello!" Woah. There's sound coming from me? Nevermind that, the hiker tells me: "Child, be wary of the waves. Not of the sea, but the waves of—" He suddenly tripped and fell, and went down the mountain. I heard a scream, but upon looking down, I didn't see the city nor the man anymore. I saw a giant chasm, a hole, that stretched deep below the ground. It was there that I saw those eyes, those red eyes, and those eyes brought me down, down into the chasm, down into the void. The fall, it felt. 

When I opened my eyes again, I was falling again. All I saw in front of me was the ocean. A green-blue sea, with ferocious waves, the textures aligning with a sense of nostalgia. A light blue sky with white clouds. I was feeling the fall strongly this time. I accelerated deep into the ocean. The impact hit me hard. It hurt so much, but what was worse was the freeze that I felt throughout my body. My orange shirt was soaked. My brown pants too, and my skin was cold. Deeply cold. I tried to gasp for air, but I couldn't even make a sound. I was submerged down further and further. I tried to breathe, but I failed. I kept trying to will my way up, but it failed. I reached out; I waited. I cried. I felt so much. My memories raced through my head. But the fire ran out. The waves got to it first.

**Labels**
My head hurts. I struggle to open up my eyes, but I managed to. It was different this time. I distinctly remember falling down, and feeling so much...pain. But I'm fine here. It's an old classroom, in the dark of night. The world feels somewhat nostalgic, but there's something missing. It's like the world with the chasm. I feel the air though. I feel myself. I feel my own thoughts. The thoughts come racing down. Too many sensations that I'm feeling. What are these sensations? I notice something about me; my shoes are orange. Orange persists in me. Why? I stand up, using my arm to help myself. I look at the chalkboard, and there's only one word written, in bright orange: "Congratulations." What does that mean? I go up to the chalkboard to examine, see if there are any traces left of the person who wrote that. All I see in the chalkboard, though, are memories. Though it's strangely blank, except the Congratulations, there are so many memories embedded into the chalkboard. I see erase marks, of words written before. I reach for the eraser, but a thought comes to me. The chalkboard. The waves. Congratulations. Congratulations? That feels familiar. I dust my hands off after holding the chalk, and place it back down. I go through the door, expecting another world, but I'm met with a hallway. An empty hallway. The floor is dirty, the ceiling lights slightly imbalanced. Suddenly, I hear a sound: "Who's there?" I say. I hear footsteps, coming down, and before I can see the source, I run for the other door. This time, I escaped the world. 

Down here, there are a long flight of...something. It doesn't seem to end. It's blank; only the framework of it are there. Scared, I step onto it once, and I feel the sensation of it. It feels weird, and It's still blank, and there is nothing else, but the sensation that I touch is there. I step down again, and this time, the form appears. They're stairs. The stairs now are the shape of stairs, and I feel the stairs. I step down again, and this time, and I see the color of the stairs come into view: brown. It's a distinct, pure brown, one solid color. I step again, and I hear the stairs suddenly makes a creak. Another step. The stair gains texture. The brown is no longer pure; there are small blemishes, small differences that construct the stairs. Another step. The stairs don't change, but I begin to taste the air around me. The atmosphere. It tastes weird. Another step. The air has a small now. It smells strangely nice. I try to climb down the stairs, but the wall opens its mouth to me: "What do the stairs mean to you? When did you become comfortable with the stairs? Think. The waves took you in, and you see now the bareness. Think of them. The labels." It let out a loud roar, with some spit hitting my orange hat. The wall's mouth then closes. I blink. And blink again. Labels? I try to ignore it, but as I walk down the stairs, I start noticing the stairs more and more. It's starting to hurt. The questions the wall asked me begin to ring in my ears as I run down the flight of stairs, the thoughts growing ever so paramount. I feel eyes, ears, hands, tongues, and noses all throw themselves at me, and the thoughts yell. The stairs yell. I'm scared. I run and run. They grow louder and louder, before the stairs themselves and the noise begins to drown themselves out. I look up and see the red eyes once again before unknowingly going through a door.
