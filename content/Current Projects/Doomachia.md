*Doomachia*, as you may know, is my current web novel project. Here are some of the following details: 

**Narrative Details:**  
- **Genre:** Science Fantasy, Psychological Thriller, War Drama

- **Tone:** High-concept, metaphysical, visceral, emotional, and existential, ultimately hopeful and bittersweet

- **Themes:** A deconstruction of binary worldviews, the idea that there are no true villains, the cycle of trauma and a metaphysical representation of mental illness, and tackling the nature of existence itself. 

- **Atmosphere:** Dark, with a focus on cosmic and existential dread, although ultimately progressive and hopeful.

**Sci-Fi Details:**    

- **Core Concept:** Chirality (left-handed, right-handed chirality, hybrids)

- **Power System:** Advanced technology; "source" manipulation

- **Key Conflict:** The World's struggle against its fundamental observer to end the cycle of existence.

- **Setting:** A tri-universal multiverse; multiple iterations of the multiverse.

- **Main Trope:** The Eternal Return

**Content Warning:** Please keep in mind that *Doomachia* explores heavy themes, including:

- **Mental Health:** Visceral depictions of mental breakdowns, severe clinical depression, anxiety, and PTSD.

- **Trauma:** Detailed explorations of childhood abuse (and sexual abuse), grief, and the psychological toll of war.

- **Violence:** Graphic depictions of war, mass casualties, and existential dread.

If you are easily triggered by any of these themes, please have this serve as a warning.

Currently, only a draft is out right now! I'm still in the writing/drafting phases, so please be aware that it's quite sloppy and not finished yet. I appreciate any feedback though!
